# 📊 Power BI Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Overview

This project presents an interactive **Power BI Sales Analysis Dashboard** designed to analyze and visualize key aspects of business performance.

The dashboard provides insights into:

* Sales performance
* Profitability
* Customer behavior
* Product performance
* Regional performance
* Shipping methods
* Monthly sales trends

The goal of the project is to transform sales data into meaningful business insights that can support **data-driven decision-making**.

---

## 🎯 Dashboard Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

* Monitor overall sales and profit performance
* Analyze sales and profit across different regions
* Identify high-performing product categories and sub-categories
* Identify top customers and products
* Analyze shipping methods
* Understand monthly sales trends
* Identify areas requiring business improvement
* Support better business and marketing decisions

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 🌎 Regional Performance

The **West region** recorded the highest sales and profit, while the **South region** recorded the lowest sales and profit.

This indicates an opportunity to improve sales and marketing performance in the South region.

### 💻 Product Performance

The **Technology** category generated the highest sales.

Among the sub-categories, **Copiers** generated the highest profit.

Some products generated negative profit, suggesting the need to review pricing, discounts, and product strategies.

### 👥 Customer Analysis

The dashboard identifies the **Top 10 Customers** based on sales contribution.

Customer retention strategies such as loyalty programs and personalized promotions can help maintain revenue growth.

### 🚚 Shipping Performance

**Standard Class** accounted for the highest proportion of sales, making it the most preferred shipping method among customers.

### 📅 Monthly Sales Trend

Sales generally increased toward the end of the year, with **November and December** showing peak sales.

This indicates seasonal demand and highlights the importance of inventory planning before the year-end sales period.

---

## 💡 Business Insights

The analysis highlights several important business insights:

* The **South region** requires additional marketing and sales initiatives.
* **Technology** is the strongest-performing product category.
* Higher discounts do not always result in higher profitability.
* A relatively small group of customers contributes a significant portion of sales.
* Seasonal demand increases toward the end of the year.

---

## 🚀 Recommendations

Based on the dashboard analysis:

1. Increase marketing campaigns and promotional activities in the **South region**.
2. Expand the **Technology** category by increasing inventory and introducing new products.
3. Review products generating negative profit and optimize pricing and discount strategies.
4. Strengthen loyalty programs for high-value customers.
5. Improve inventory planning before the year-end sales season.
